CLINICAL NOTES

References & safety

  • Calculations are intended as quick clinical aids, not as substitutes for clinical judgement.
  • Always verify medication-specific maximum doses, concentrations, intervals and local prescribing guidance.
  • Pediatric interpretation often depends on age, sex, growth charts and the complete clinical context.
  • Fluid calculations should be adjusted according to hydration, perfusion, renal function, cardiac status, electrolytes and ongoing losses.
  • Airway formulas are estimates and tube placement must always be clinically confirmed.
  • Laboratory reference intervals can vary by laboratory, analyzer and patient population.
Important: This website is a calculation companion only. It does not diagnose disease, replace clinical examination, or provide patient-specific medical advice. Always use current institutional protocols and appropriate professional judgement.
