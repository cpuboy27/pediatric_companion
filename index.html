References & clinical note
This tool is intended as a calculation aid and does not replace clinical judgement, prescribing references, institutional protocols, age-specific reference ranges, or specialist advice.
  • Verify every calculated dose, concentration, maximum dose and interval against the relevant drug reference.
  • Pediatric fluid calculations should always be interpreted with the child's clinical condition, hydration status, electrolytes and renal/cardiac status.
  • Growth and laboratory calculations should be interpreted using appropriate pediatric reference standards.
  • Airway calculations are estimates and tube placement must always be clinically confirmed.
